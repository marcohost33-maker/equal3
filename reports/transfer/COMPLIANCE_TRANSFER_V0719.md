# Equal3 v0.7.19 – compliance transfer (proposal, not central matrix edit)

**Risk:** persistence of root/deployment IDs, request/rotation metadata at a remote witness can constitute linkable service metadata. For a transparency log, published digests can enable linkability even without plaintext.

**Controls requiring an owner:** separation of Swiss/equivalent data protection roles; explicit controller/processor and data processing agreements; data minimization of witness payload (no documents/personal data); asset/key inventory, access logging and MFA; retention and incident procedures; Swiss DSG risk assessment/DSFA where applicable; deletion-vs-immutability trade-off for public logs; third-party service jurisdiction; cryptographic backup deletion policy. This file is only an input for compliance matrix review, not evidence that any policy was enacted.
